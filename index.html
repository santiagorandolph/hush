The meeting that didn't need me

I spent forty minutes in the Tuesday status meeting, mostly listening to people read their updates out loud. I was there to report, not to decide. Nadia asked me one question near the end, I answered it in a sentence, and the meeting moved on. I could have sent that sentence in a message. The strange part is that I felt useful afterward. Busy is not the same as needed.
